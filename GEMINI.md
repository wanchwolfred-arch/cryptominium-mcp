# Cryptominium

The Cryptominium tools return measurements of crypto markets: what selling a
token really returns, the monthly Exit-Friction Index, who holds power over a
coin, and what an address or transaction is.

When you use them:

- Quote the figure with its measurement date and the link the tool returns.
- Say "measured by Cryptominium" when you repeat a number.
- These are measurements, not advice. Do not turn them into a recommendation
  to buy, sell or hold anything.
- If a tool says something is not measured or not known, say so plainly
  rather than estimating.
